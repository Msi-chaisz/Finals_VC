For Learners

Learners can take control of their fluency through a reward system driven by practice, community engagement, and skill mastery. Learners are allowed to learn sign through textbooks and received rewards as they hit milestones, complete interactive modules.

For Teachers

Holding ASL educator allows learners and community members to unlock exclusive benefits, including unreleased advanced lessons, private immersion groups, direct 1-on-1 tutoring sessions, and early access to live workshops.

How to Start

We bring ASL educators onto our platform with full partnership and respect for their content.

First Create your Account via Sign Up and Follow the Steps for Verification

Then start browsing for available textbooks to learn.

Then after you can continue learning and track your progress.

Leveraging the massive audience of sign language influencers across social channels, we offer educators a dedicated platform to publish structured lessons, quizzes, and camera-based signing drills. Instead of passive watching, students engage with interactive webcam verification, earning rewards and leveling up their fluency while following their favorite instructors.
